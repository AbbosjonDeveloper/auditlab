Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before running your audit

Is this really free? What's the catch?
Yes, 100% free. No email gate, no upsell. The audit is our way of introducing you to data-driven marketing — we may offer paid services later, but the audit itself will always be free.
How accurate are the budget estimates?
We use platform-specific CPL (cost-per-lead) ranges based on 2024-2025 industry benchmarks across Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, and TikTok. Your real numbers will depend on targeting, creative quality, and offer — but the ranges we give are solid starting points.
What if my industry isn't listed?
Select "Other" — the funnel math and budget calculations still apply universally. The tips will be more general but the core numbers will still be accurate for your goal.
Can I share or save my audit results?
Yes. On the results page you can print or save as PDF using your browser's built-in print function. We're working on a shareable link feature for a future update.
Do I need to be a marketing expert to understand the results?
Not at all. We explain every number in plain English — what it means, why it matters, and what to do about it. If you know your revenue goal and your average sale, you're ready.
What if I don't know my conversion rate?
Start with an estimate: online businesses average 2-5%, service businesses 20-40%, high-ticket B2B 10-20%. You can run the audit multiple times with different numbers to see how your budget shifts.
