# AuditLab — Marketing & Budget Audit

A free, data-driven marketing audit tool. Answer 9 questions and get a complete marketing roadmap with funnel math, ad budget recommendations, risk analysis, and industry-specific tips.

## Features

- **Multi-step wizard** — 9 quick questions, one screen at a time
- **Overall grade** — A+ to F based on your marketing readiness
- **Funnel visualization** — Impressions → Leads → Customers → Revenue
- **Budget breakdown** — Min / Optimal / Real (with risk multiplier)
- **Industry-specific tips** — 16 industries covered
- **Shareable results** — Copy link and share
- **Fully responsive** — Works on mobile, tablet, desktop

## Tech

Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies.

## Local development

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open http://localhost:8000

## Pages

- `index.html` — Landing page
- `audit.html` — 9-question wizard
- `results.html` — Personalized audit report
